## Can Target coupons be stacked?

Sometimes, but the exact combination depends on the offer type.

Target says one manufacturer coupon, one Target category offer, and one Target item-level offer can generally be combined per item when the terms allow it. Manufacturer coupons must match the product description, including details such as brand, size, quantity, color, or flavor.

A Target coupon and a manufacturer coupon are different things:

- A **manufacturer coupon** is issued by the product manufacturer.
- A **Target coupon** is issued by Target.
- A **Target Circle deal** is a member-linked offer that may apply automatically or require activation, depending on the offer.
- A **promo code** is entered manually during online checkout.
- A **gift card promotion** gives a future-use Target GiftCard after a qualifying purchase.

Target does not accept expired codes, codes for products it does not carry, counterfeit coupons, or codes that do not match the product description. It also does not apply coupon value above the reduced price of the item.

For online orders, the discount should appear in the order summary before you submit payment. If the cart does not show the saving, do not assume it will be corrected after checkout.
